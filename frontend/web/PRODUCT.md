# Product

## Register

product

## Users

All three roles use the system equally:

- **Cashiers (Kasir)**: Front-line staff at the counter processing orders, handling payments, managing daily tasks, printing receipts. Need speed and simplicity. Working under time pressure during peak hours.
- **Admin Staff**: Managers overseeing operations, employee scheduling, service configuration, booking confirmations. Need overview and control across the operation.
- **Owners**: Business owners reviewing financial reports, performance metrics, and operational insights. Need data-driven visibility for strategic decisions.

## Product Purpose

LuxeWash is a comprehensive POS and management system for Laundaja Group's laundromat chain. It streamlines daily operations across multiple roles—from transaction processing at the counter to executive reporting.

Success looks like: reduced transaction time at the counter, clear operational visibility for managers, and confident business decisions through data for owners.

## Brand Personality

**Modern & Premium**: Polished, refined, high-end feel matching the "Luxe" positioning. Glassmorphic interfaces with frosted glass cards, deep royal blue sidebars, and metallic gold accents convey quality through transparency and depth. The system should feel like a premium financial dashboard, not a generic utility.

**3-word personality**: Refined, Efficient, Trustworthy

## Anti-references

- **SAP/Oracle enterprise UI**: Overly complex forms, overwhelming data density, steep learning curve. Avoid pattern overload.
- **Generic Bootstrap admin**: Blue sidebar, chart-heavy dashboards, template feel. The system should feel bespoke with glassmorphic depth, not purchased.
- **Over-decorated SaaS**: Gratuitous motion, display fonts in labels, invented affordances. Earn familiarity through glass transparency, don't fight it.

## Design Principles

1. **Simplicity for Staff**: Every workflow should be completable in minimal steps. Reduce cognitive load for busy counter staff with clear hierarchies and obvious actions. The tool disappears into the task.
2. **Data-Driven Insights**: Surface the right data at the right time. Dashboards and reports should drive confident business decisions, not just display numbers.
3. **Premium Polish**: Every interaction should feel refined and intentional. Glassmorphic cards, soft gradients, and typography should convey quality—earned familiarity through transparency, not template default.
4. **Role-Appropriate Clarity**: Each role sees exactly what they need—nothing more, nothing less. Information hierarchy adapts to context and task.
5. **Operational Confidence**: The system should reduce anxiety at the counter. Clear status indicators, obvious next steps, and reliable feedback loops build trust.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance throughout. Specific considerations:

- Sufficient color contrast for all text (4.5:1 minimum for body text, 3:1 for large text)
- Keyboard navigation for all interactive elements
- Clear focus states on all actionable elements
- Screen reader compatibility for critical workflows (transactions, status changes)
- Reduced motion alternatives for all animations
